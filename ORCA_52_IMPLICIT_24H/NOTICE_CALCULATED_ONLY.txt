THIS PACKAGE PRODUCES CALCULATED PREDICTIONS, NOT EXPERIMENTAL DATA.

The ORCA calculations predict isotropic nuclear shieldings. Chemical shifts are
referenced to matching calculated TMS/TSP jobs. Proton multiplet structure is a
time-bounded empirical geometry/topology model unless a result explicitly says
SELECTIVE_DFT_J. Noise, baseline, solvent, water/HOD peaks and FID-like data are
synthetic acquisition appearance. HRMS is an intact formula/adduct exact-mass
and natural-isotope calculation; it does not predict fragmentation or measured
ion abundance.

These files may be used for method development, experiment planning and model
evaluation. They must not be submitted or represented as measured supporting
information. Replace predictions with actual instrument data for publication.
