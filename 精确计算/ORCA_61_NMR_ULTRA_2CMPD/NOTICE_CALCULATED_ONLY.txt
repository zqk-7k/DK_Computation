THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS CALCULATED MODELS AND CALCULATION INPUTS ONLY.

Nothing generated by ORCA, xTB, the scheduler, or the post-processing scripts
is an acquired experimental NMR or mass spectrum. Synthetic acquisition
appearance (noise, solvent peaks, water peaks, line broadening, or baseline)
must remain labelled SYNTHETIC/CALCULATED NOT EXPERIMENTAL.

ORCA and xTB binaries are not distributed in this folder. Install and license
them separately under the terms of their respective providers.

The formula-level HRMS branch predicts exact masses and isotope envelopes. It
does not predict validated collision-induced fragmentation, chromatograms, or
experimental "found" m/z values.
