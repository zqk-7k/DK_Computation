CALCULATED NMR WORKFLOW — NOT EXPERIMENTAL DATA

This package prepares and processes ORCA calculations for 51 compounds. It does
not contain experimental confirmation unless a file is explicitly labelled as an
external experimental input with provenance.

CLEAN_CALCULATED outputs contain calculated shieldings/shifts, calculated or
assumed scalar couplings, assignments and idealized line shapes.

SYNTHETIC_APPEARANCE outputs may add user-specified line widths, peak amplitudes,
solvent/residual-water peaks, noise, baseline and other display effects. Those
features are post-processing assumptions. Static first-principles ORCA calculations
do not determine a real instrument baseline, noise realization, receiver response,
phasing, concentration-dependent intensity, exchange broadening or all peak widths.

PBE0 and revDSD raw shieldings are separate method branches and must not be mixed.
PBE/pcJ-3 and PBE0/pcJ-2 coupling branches are separate and must not be averaged.
Different fixed explicit-solvent compositions, protonation states, salt states,
charges or multiplicities are separate models and must not be Boltzmann averaged
together.

The usual 2 + 3 + 5 GOAT success counts are minimum first-pass targets, not proof
of conformational convergence. Every production result requires passed identity,
composition, stereochemistry, coverage, thermochemistry, reference, calibration,
method and provenance gates.

No file in this package should be described as "validated", "experimental", or
"guaranteed exact" unless the corresponding independent validation evidence and
gate receipt are present.
